title: "About us"
---

Senior News Bureau (SNB) is an independent news program for older adults in the United States. Every morning and evening we publish two editions:

- **Senior News**: practical health, money and safety news for everyday life.
- **Past News**: stories from the decades our viewers remember.

Each story comes with the broadcast video from our channel. Our articles are prepared with the help of AI tools and reviewed before publication.

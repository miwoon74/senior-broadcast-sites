You do not need a gym to stay active. A regular walk is one of the simplest habits for keeping your legs strong and your balance steady.

Start with a distance that feels easy, and add a few minutes each week. Walking with a friend or a group makes it easier to keep going, and gives you a reason to get out of the house on gray days.

If you have heart, joint or balance concerns, ask your doctor what pace and distance are right for you.

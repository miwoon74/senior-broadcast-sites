How can older adults enjoy the festive beauty of world-class holiday displays without facing freezing winter winds, long walking paths, or slippery surfaces? At Callaway Resort and Gardens in Pine Mountain, Georgia, the answer lies along a tranquil forest path illuminated by **10 million lights**. The resort's annual Fantasy in Lights celebration has officially opened for the 2026–2027 winter season, providing retirees and their families an expansive holiday tradition designed for comfort, physical accessibility, and relaxed pacing. 

With admission structured across flexible pricing tiers starting at **$24.95** for Value Nights, older travelers can plan a memorable seasonal outing that accommodates physical comfort while keeping seasonal expenses manageable.

## Seeing the Display: Scale, Schedule, and Pricing by the Numbers

Now celebrating its 34th annual showcase, the Fantasy in Lights display has long earned national recognition as one of the country's premier winter spectacles. The 2026 production opened on November 13, 2026, and welcomes visitors nightly through January 4, 2027.

The attraction is built around an expansive 7-mile vehicular route that winds through scenic woodland, featuring 18 custom-illuminated animated scenes. For the 2026 season, admission is sold on a per-person tiered ticketing structure rather than a single flat fee per vehicle. This approach allows smaller parties and retired couples to secure lower entry rates by choosing off-peak dates.

## Personal Vehicle vs. Jolly Trolley: Choosing the Best Option for Mobility

When attending Fantasy in Lights, visitors can experience the 18 animated scenes through two primary touring methods: driving their own personal automobile or purchasing a ride on the resort's open-air Jolly Trolley.

For seniors managing chronic back pain, joint stiffness, or respiratory sensitivity to cold night air, the personal vehicle option provides distinct health and mobility advantages:

1. **Customized Climate Control:** Winter evening temperatures can drop rapidly in wooded areas. Touring inside your own car allows you to adjust the heater, fans, and seat warmers to match your precise health needs.
2. **Ergonomic Seating:** Personal vehicles allow drivers and passengers to adjust backrests and lumbar supports, avoiding the upright wooden or composite bench seating found on open-air group trolleys.
3. **Pacing and Sensory Comfort:** Inside a personal automobile, travelers can regulate interior volume, listen to their own preferred holiday music, or pause briefly in pull-off bays without feeling crowded by large touring groups.

For guests who prefer an open-air group atmosphere, the Jolly Trolley operates along the same path. However, older visitors choosing the trolley should dress in heavy winter layers, bring wind-resistant head coverings, and pack warm lap blankets to manage sustained exposure to moving night air.

## Visiting the Christmas Village: Paving, Restrooms, and Timing

In addition to the 7-mile driving trail, admission includes access to the Callaway Christmas Village. For older adults who enjoy holiday shopping and hot seasonal beverages, navigating large festival markets often raises concerns about tripping hazards, unpaved gravel paths, or inadequate seating.

The facilities at Pine Mountain address these accessibility requirements through intentional design:

- **Surface Grading and Pavements:** The Christmas Village features level, paved pedestrian walkways that accommodate walking canes, walkers, and wheelchairs without the hazard of loose gravel, wet grass, or tree roots.
- **Accessible Parking and ADA Facilities:** Designated accessible parking bays are placed close to the central village pavilion. The resort maintains fully compliant ADA restroom facilities adjacent to the main dining and shopping spaces.
- **Heated Seating Zones:** Heated rest areas are stationed within the village, offering resting spots where seniors can sit comfortably and warm up before boarding vehicles.

Timing your arrival can make a significant difference in physical comfort. The Christmas Village opens its doors daily at 4:00 PM, while vehicles and trolley departures along the light trail begin at dusk, around 6:00 PM. Arriving at 4:00 PM allows older guests to park conveniently near the pavilion in full daylight, browse the village shops, and finish an early dinner without navigating dense evening crowds. As dusk settles around 6:00 PM, visitors can transition directly into their warm personal cars to start the 7-mile drive.

Are you looking for a festive, low-impact outdoor outing that accommodates limited mobility without sacrificing holiday cheer? The Missouri Botanical Garden launched its 2026 Garden Glow celebration in St. Louis, featuring **over 2,000,000 decorative lights** along an accessible pathway designed specifically for a comfortable seasonal stroll.

Shorter daylight hours and colder temperatures often lead to indoor isolation and reduced physical activity for older adults during the winter months. Public light exhibits offer a refreshing way to stay active, social, and connected with family. Before you head out into the evening chill, understanding the physical layout, ticket pricing tiers, and on-site accessibility accommodations can make the difference between an exhausting trip and an enjoyable seasonal memory.

## The Event by the Numbers: Trails, Lights, and Admission Costs

Garden Glow runs on select dates from November 14, 2026, through January 2, 2027, at the historic 4344 Shaw Boulevard campus in St. Louis. To ensure that older visitors and multigenerational families can navigate the grounds safely after dark, the garden routes the entire outdoor installation along a flat, fully paved loop.

The display provides continuous illumination across the path, which spans 1.0 mile and typically takes between 45 to 60 minutes to complete at a gentle walking pace.

The numbers behind the 2026 installation illustrate the scale and pacing of the display:

- **2,000,000+ lights:** More than two million twinkling lights illuminate the grounds, creating a well-lit environment that improves ground visibility compared to standard nighttime outdoor trails.
- **1.0-mile paved loop:** The primary route is completely paved and barrier-free, eliminating loose gravel, tree roots, or unpaved dirt trails.
- **45 to 60 minutes:** The recommended walking loop time allows for an unhurried, low-impact stroll.
- **$6 to $25 ticket range:** Advance admission prices scale across dates, days of the week, and membership status, offering lower costs for budget-conscious attendees.
- **$2 late booking increase:** Purchasing tickets same-day after 4:30 p.m. adds $2 per ticket compared to advance reservations.

## Planning for Physical Comfort and Accessibility

When attending an outdoor winter evening event, accessibility and warmth are essential considerations. A nighttime walk requires clear sightlines, reliable paving for assistive mobility devices, and heated indoor options along the way.

The Missouri Botanical Garden provides several structural accommodations to assist seniors and visitors with limited mobility. Complimentary manual wheelchairs are offered on a first-come, first-served basis at the Ridgway Visitor Center near the entrance. In addition, designated accessible parking is available near the entrance pavilion.

Similar botanical events nationwide offer targeted accommodations for older adults. For example, the Gardens Aglow event in Maine also opened this week with senior discounts starting at $18. For year-round natural visits beyond seasonal holiday lights, older travelers can also consider the America the Beautiful Lifetime Senior Pass, available for $80 through the federal store.

## How to Save Money on Admission

Visiting seasonal displays with children, grandchildren, or friends can become costly if tickets are purchased at the gate. Advance planning provides immediate financial savings.

Advance tickets for the 2026 Garden Glow season range from $6 to $25 per person. Because ticket pricing scales depending on peak dates and weekdays, scheduling a visit on an off-peak weekday evening generally secures the lowest available rates. 

Furthermore, booking your timed-entry reservation online directly through missouribotanicalgarden.org avoids the $2 price increase applied to tickets purchased same-day after 4:30 p.m. Online booking also guarantees entry, preventing the inconvenience of arriving at the gate only to find a specific time slot sold out.

## Senior Checklist: Preparing for an Accessible Holiday Light Walk

Before leaving home for an evening botanical light walk, review these practical steps to ensure a safe, comfortable, and budget-friendly outing:

- ☐ Reserve timed-entry passes online in advance at missouribotanicalgarden.org to avoid the $2 same-day price increase after 4:30 p.m.
- ☐ Aim for an early evening arrival around opening time (approximately 5:00 p.m.) to secure accessible parking and complimentary manual wheelchairs at the Visitor Center before supplies run out.
- ☐ Wear sturdy, non-slip walking shoes and dress in warm layers, including gloves and a scarf, to stay comfortable during the 45-to-60-minute loop.
- ☐ Locate heated stopping points on the map, including the Emerson Conservatory and heated indoor pavilions, to take warmth breaks as needed.
- ☐ Plan for resting bench stops along the 1-mile route so you do not have to rush your walk.
- ☐ If you need assistance finding community transportation or local senior recreation programs in your home area, contact the federal Eldercare Locator at 1-800-677-1116 or visit eldercare.acl.gov.

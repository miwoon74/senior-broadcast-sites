How can older adults enjoy the holiday light season while avoiding steep weekend crowds and high peak ticket costs? The Denver Botanic Gardens opened its signature annual winter light show, Blossoms of Light, at its historic York Street location on Friday, November 20, 2026. For this season, the garden introduced a dynamic plan-ahead pricing model where individual admission ranges from **$19 to $45**, offering significant cost savings for visitors who plan their visit during off-peak evenings. 

The outdoor exhibition runs nightly from 4:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. through January 10, 2027. Combining expansive illuminated displays with accessible pathways and dedicated accommodations, the event serves as an accessible seasonal outing for older adults and multigenerational families.

## Understanding the 2026 Plan-Ahead Pricing Model

For the 2026–2027 run, the Denver Botanic Gardens implemented plan-ahead pricing to balance visitor traffic and encourage visits during non-peak hours. Admission rates are tied directly to the day of the week and advance purchase timing.

Quiet weeknight tickets begin as low as $19, while base adult tickets start at $24. High-demand slots—particularly peak weekend evenings closer to the holidays—climb up to $45. 

For older couples or families attending together, scheduling flexibility creates immediate budget benefits. Choosing a Tuesday or Wednesday evening reservation rather than a Saturday peak slot can save a couple over $30 in general admission alone. Furthermore, special discounted rates are provided for seniors aged 65 and older, garden members, and military personnel. 

All guests must secure a timed-entry reservation online before arriving at the gates, as walk-up ticket sales are restricted to preserve capacity limits and maintain crowd flow.

## Accessibility Features at the York Street Gardens

Navigating public events during chilly winter evenings can present mobility concerns, but the York Street grounds feature infrastructure tailored for older visitors, wheelchair users, and those with limited mobility.

The outdoor display features wide, flat, fully paved trails designed to meet ADA accessibility standards. The walkways accommodate standard wheelchairs, power chairs, walking frames, and strollers, reducing slip or trip hazards on unpaved ground. Frequent bench seating is situated throughout the illuminated route, offering natural rest stops for guests who prefer to pace their stroll.

Getting to the venue has also become simpler this season. Visitors utilizing public transportation can take advantage of the newly opened East Colfax transit station at York Street, reducing parking congestion and walking distances from regional bus or rail connections.

## Scheduling Strategies to Avoid Crowds and Peak Costs

Selecting the right evening makes a substantial difference in both personal comfort and overall cost. Peak weekend evenings typically draw large crowds, creating tighter conditions along the display paths. In contrast, early-week sessions offer quieter walking conditions, easier access to rest benches, and unobstructed views of the lighting arrangements.

Key practical steps for visiting:

- **Target Mid-Week Slots:** Selecting Tuesday or Wednesday entry windows yields the lowest rates near $19, while keeping foot traffic manageable.
- **Select Earlier Entry Times:** Booking a slot right at 4:30 p.m. allows you to enter just as twilight begins, ensuring ample time to complete the loop before late-evening crowds gather.
- **Utilize Public Transit:** Seniors traveling via the Denver Regional Transportation District (RTD) can inquire about senior discount fares for bus and rail lines connecting to York Street.
- **Arrange Special Accommodations in Advance:** If personalized mobility accommodations are required, call visitor services at 720-865-3500 before your scheduled reservation.

## Essential Preparation Checklist for Older Adults

- ☐ Book timed-entry tickets online through BotanicGardens.org well in advance to secure senior admission and off-peak pricing
- ☐ Choose a Tuesday or Wednesday timeslot between 4:30 p.m. and 9:00 p.m. to avoid weekend congestion
- ☐ Verify weather forecasts and layer clothing appropriately for an outdoor evening walk
- ☐ Bring trekking poles fitted with rubber tips if additional walking stability is needed on paved walkways
- ☐ Check wheelchair rental availability at the Bonfils-Stanton Visitor Center if assistance is required
- ☐ Review RTD public transit routes stopping at the newly opened East Colfax transit station at York Street

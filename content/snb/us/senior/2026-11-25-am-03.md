Could your monthly retirement deposit be smaller than what you legally earned over your working life? According to federal accounting disclosures, the Social Security Administration underpaid American beneficiaries by an estimated **$2 billion** in fiscal year 2025. 

Administrative miscalculations, clerical gaps, and missing lifetime earnings records left these uncollected funds sitting on government balance sheets. Discrepancies were documented across old-age retirement, Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI), and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) programs. For seniors managing daily expenses under persistent inflation, auditing your official wage history is an essential step to ensure you receive every dollar you are owed.

## Why Missing Earnings and Administrative Errors Occur

Benefit calculation errors rarely stem from deliberate actions; rather, they arise from systemic data mismatches between employers, federal tax agencies, and Social Security Administration databases. 

The primary causes identified in the fiscal 2025 accounting audit include:

- **Unlinked W-2 Filings:** Employers submit annual wage filings to the government, but typographical errors in names or mismatched Social Security numbers prevent wage credits from posting to an employee's permanent record.
- **Unprocessed Self-Employment Earnings:** Independent contractors and small business owners report net earnings via IRS Form 1040 Schedule SE. Delays or misalignments between internal tax processing and Social Security records leave some working years showing zero dollars.
- **Delays in Recalculating Post-Retirement Earnings:** Retirees who return to work or maintain part-time employment often generate earnings that replace lower-earning years in their record, and recalculations can be delayed.earning historical years among their top 35 years. Automatic upward recalculations frequently face administrative backlogs.
- **Unreconciled Retirement Earnings Test Deductions:** Early claimants subject to the annual earnings test—capped at $24,480 in 2026 and projected at $25,560 in 2027—often see monthly checks withheld. When those workers reach full retirement age, the law mandates a recalculation to restore withheld amounts over time, yet clerical backlogs can stall these repayments.
- **Outdated Government Pension Offsets:** Public-sector employees affected by statutory pension offsets sometimes have their benefit offsets calculated with outdated figures, depressing their net monthly deposits.

## How to Audit Your Lifetime Wage Ledger

Reviewing your earnings history can be done from home through official government web portals and generally does not require processing fees.als. Beneficiaries maintain a statutory right to request a formal review and adjustment of their Primary Insurance Amount whenever historical records show uncredited compensation.

First, log into your personal account at ssa.gov/myaccount to view and download your complete lifetime earnings statement. This document presents a line-by-line summary of every calendar year you worked, listing the exact wage total recorded under your Social Security number.

Second, compare your historical income tax paperwork, such as old Form W-2 slips or IRS Form 1040 returns with Schedule SE attachments, against the figures displayed on your statement. Pay specific attention to any calendar year where you worked and paid Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA) taxes but your official statement displays zero dollars or an unusually low wage sum.

Third, if you locate a discrepancy, prepare to submit Form SSA-7008, titled Request for Correction of Earnings Record. You must provide physical or digital copies of proof of earnings, such as tax filings or wage statements from the employer in question, to document the correct amount.

Fourth, you can schedule an appointment with your local Social Security field office or contact the national agency helpline directly at 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778) to initiate an administrative file review. For retirees seeking independent review assistance regarding their benefits and interactions with Medicare programs, the State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) offers free regional guidance online at shiphelp.org or via phone at 1-877-839-2675. Benefits counselors advise reviewing your online earnings record before Friday, December 4, to ensure adequate time to locate supporting documents and request year-end corrections.

## Action Plan for Beneficiaries

Use this checklist to verify your benefit records and claim uncredited earnings:

- ☐ Log into your verified account at ssa.gov/myaccount and download your full lifetime earnings record.
- ☐ Verify that each working year has an accurate dollar amount listed rather than an uncredited zero.
- ☐ Cross-examine flagged years against old IRS Form W-2 statements or Form 1040 Schedule SE tax records.
- ☐ Complete Form SSA-7008 (Request for Correction of Earnings Record) if any uncredited income is detected.
- ☐ Contact the SSA toll-free at 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778) or visit a local field office with documentation.
- ☐ Consult a SHIP counselor at shiphelp.org or 1-877-839-2675 for free independent guidance on benefit and Medicare interactions.

## How to evaluate a town before you move
Choosing a retirement destination affects more than sticker price. Use these categories to compare places you’re considering:

- Health access: distance to hospitals and availability of Medicare‑accepting primary care.
- Long‑term and in‑home care: presence of home‑health agencies and assisted living options.
- Transportation: local transit, on‑demand services, walkability of downtowns.
- Housing costs and property tax: not just sales price but ongoing taxes and insurance in that state/county.
- Community supports: senior centers, meal programs, volunteer networks.

## Practical checklist before you relocate
- ☐ Visit the town for at least a week to check weather, services and daily routines.
- ☐ Call local hospitals and clinics to confirm Medicare acceptance and wait times.
- ☐ Contact Eldercare Locator at 1-800-677-1116 or https://eldercare.acl.gov to find local aging services.
- ☐ Review property tax and insurance estimates for the exact town or county.
- ☐ Meet or call local senior centers, transportation providers, and home‑care agencies.
- ☐ Consider a home‑safety assessment for stairs, bathroom modifications and single‑level living.

## Why local research matters: services and costs vary
The briefing emphasizes that availability of medical services and long‑term care "varies widely by town." That means two Poconos towns only a short drive apart can differ in whether they have an urgent care, a full hospital, or nearby in‑home care providers. Local government pages, county health departments and state licensing lists are the places to find those specifics.

## Steps for follow‑up research (where to get the numbers)
1. Check the WorldAtlas town page for each named town to find any listed median housing costs and local details.
2. Use U.S. Census migration tables for 2020–2025 to see who is moving in and out of the county you’re considering.
3. Contact county assessor and treasurer offices for current property‑tax rates.
4. Call hospitals and clinics in the town for provider lists and Medicare participation.
5. Use Eldercare Locator (https://eldercare.acl.gov or 1-800-677-1116) to find area agencies on aging and local home‑care resources.

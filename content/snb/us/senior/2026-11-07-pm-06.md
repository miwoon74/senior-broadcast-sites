## Practical things you can do (simple, safe, practical)

- Ask and listen: Start by asking your grandchild what causes matter to them. Let them explain—no judgment, just curiosity.
- One tangible action to offer: Assemble a small care package of menstrual or hygiene items and drop it at a local school, shelter, or community center.
- Volunteer together: Offer to help at a local drive or donate supplies; small shared tasks build connection.
- Learn local rules: If you plan to donate to schools or shelters, check the receiving organization’s guidelines first (contact them or a local United Way or community center).
- Support without taking over: Offer help for one concrete task (buying supplies, driving to a volunteer event, funding a small purchase) rather than expecting to lead a campaign.

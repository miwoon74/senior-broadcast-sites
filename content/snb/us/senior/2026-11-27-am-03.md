As retirees plan their personal budgets for the coming year, a key question is how much of their upcoming Social Security cost-of-living adjustment will actually reach their bank accounts. Federal health officials released the official Medicare Part A and Part B cost schedule for calendar year 2027 this week, establishing that the standard monthly Part B outpatient premium will climb by $6.50 to reach **$209.40**. Because the federal government automatically deducts Part B premiums from most monthly Social Security checks, this adjustment directly shapes how much net income older Americans will take home starting in January.

## By the Numbers: Official Medicare Cost Changes for 2027

According to the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS), the upcoming 3.2 percent increase in the standard Part B outpatient premium represents a notably smaller jump than the 9.7 percent spike experienced in the prior annual cycle. However, out-of-pocket costs will climb across several service categories, including outpatient care, annual deductibles, inpatient hospital admissions, and extended skilled nursing stays.



In addition to the standard outpatient charges, the inpatient deductible required for hospital care under Part A will rise by $40 next year, bringing the cost per benefit period to $1,776. High-income beneficiaries will also see adjusted income-related monthly adjustment amounts (IRMAA), with individual surcharges beginning for single tax filers earning above $112,000.

## How Higher Part B Rates Impact Social Security Checks

For millions of seniors, Medicare costs and Social Security benefits operate as connected parts of the same household ledger. Social Security retirement benefits are scheduled to receive a 3.6 percent cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) starting in January. However, the mandatory monthly deduction for Medicare Part B will consume roughly nine percent of the average retiree's gross COLA increase.

The financial impact can be seen clearly when calculating the monthly payout for an average retired worker:

- **Estimated average monthly benefit:** roughly $1,900
- **Gross 3.6 percent monthly raise:** approximately $68.40
- **Additional Part B premium deduction:** $6.50
- **Actual net monthly increase:** $61.90

This net increase of $61.90 per month is what remains to absorb shifting living expenses, including everyday groceries, home heating and energy bills, and out-of-pocket prescription drug copayments.

## Navigating Open Enrollment and Supplemental Choices

The release of official federal rates comes during the annual Medicare Open Enrollment window, which runs through a firm national deadline of December 7. During this time, beneficiaries have the legal right to change their existing Medicare Advantage plans, switch back to Original Medicare, or adjust their Part D stand-alone prescription drug plans. 

Reviewing plan options helps beneficiaries evaluate whether their existing physicians and prescription drugs will remain covered under preferred tiers in 2027. Comparing available Medigap supplemental coverage against Medicare Advantage options also provides protection against unexpected hospital deductibles or outpatient copayments during the coming calendar year.

Beneficiaries who need assistance reviewing plan structures can access free, unbiased counseling through the national State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) network, accessible at shiphelp.org or by phone at 1-877-839-2675.

## Action Checklist for Your 2027 Healthcare Budget

- ☐ Log into your personal account at medicare.gov to compare your current drug list against 2027 Part D plan formularies
- ☐ Review your plan before the December 7 Open Enrollment deadline to verify network changes and premium adjustments
- ☐ Check whether your local doctors and specialists remain in-network under your Medicare Advantage or supplemental policy
- ☐ Evaluate whether your income falls below $23,000 annually (for individuals) to apply for the federal Extra Help program at ssa.gov
- ☐ Contact 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) or your local Medicaid office to verify eligibility for Medicare Savings Programs that can pay your Part B premiums
- ☐ Schedule a consultation with a State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) counselor at shiphelp.org or 1-877-839-2675 if you need personalized assistance

With exactly two weeks left before the federal Medicare open enrollment deadline on December 7, older Americans face critical decisions regarding their healthcare coverage for 2027. If you take no action before the window closes, your current policy will automatically roll over into next year under newly adjusted rates. Federal announcements confirm that standard Medicare Part B monthly premiums are set to climb to **$209.40**, while millions of seniors risk losing their existing coverage altogether due to widespread insurer exits. 

Taking time to examine your options today can safeguard your monthly budget and prevent unexpected coverage gaps at the pharmacy counter when the new year begins; check the latest at Medicare.gov.

## The 2027 Cost Shifts: Higher Premiums and New Out-of-Pocket Caps

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services finalized the official cost figures for the upcoming coverage year on November 17, 2026. The baseline expenses that beneficiaries pay to maintain outpatient and physician care are moving upward across the board.

Under the updated schedule, the standard monthly Medicare Part B premium will rise by $6.50, increasing from $202.90 in 2026 to $209.40 in 2027. Alongside the monthly premium hike, the annual Part B medical deductible will increase to $292.00. 



While outpatient care costs are expanding, federal reforms are also establishing a vital financial safeguard for prescription medications. For 2027, the maximum annual out-of-pocket spending cap on covered prescription drugs has been fixed at $2,400.00. This threshold establishes a legal ceiling on drug copayments, providing relief for seniors managing costly chronic conditions.

## Why 4.3 Million Seniors Face Automatic Plan Terminations

The upcoming deadline is especially urgent for an estimated 4.3 million older adults. According to research published by the nonpartisan health organization KFF, approximately 4.3 million beneficiaries are enrolled in Medicare Advantage plans or standalone Medicare Part D prescription drug plans that will officially discontinue operations by December 31.

When an insurance carrier exits a county or completely cancels a Medicare Advantage offering, beneficiaries cannot rely on standard automatic renewal. If your carrier drops your plan and you do not proactively choose a replacement by December 7, Medicare will default you back into Original Medicare. 

Defaulting into Original Medicare without planning carries serious financial hazards:
- Original Medicare does not include outpatient prescription drug coverage. You could find yourself on January 1 without any Part D prescription drug policy.
- Private insurers frequently alter provider directories, drop preferred local pharmacy networks, and modify drug formularies from year to year.
- Essential maintenance medications for conditions like diabetes or heart disease could shift into a higher copayment tier or lose coverage entirely under an unexpected default structure.

Beneficiaries whose plans are terminated do have access to a special enrollment period that extends through February 28. However, selecting a new plan by the December 7 open enrollment deadline remains essential to guarantee seamless coverage starting on January 1, 2027, avoiding pharmacy rejections or uncovered medical visits during the first weeks of the year.

## How Premium Deductions Impact Your Social Security Check

Medicare Part B premiums are deducted directly from monthly Social Security payments for the majority of retired Americans. For a beneficiary receiving the average monthly Social Security benefit of $1,900, the rising monthly Part B deduction to $209.40 reduces the net take-home benefit.

When monthly deductions rise, any cost-of-living adjustments can be quickly eroded if out-of-pocket medical expenses are left unmanaged. While the new $2,400.00 annual prescription drug cap offers essential protection against catastrophic expenses, you only benefit from this cap if your medications remain on your plan's approved formulary. 

If your plan drops a medication or moves it to a non-preferred tier, your immediate out-of-pocket costs at the pharmacy counter can escalate rapidly. Reviewing plan formularies before the December 7 deadline ensures your routine prescriptions are covered under terms that keep your copayments predictable and manageable throughout 2027.

## Practical Steps to Evaluate Your Coverage Before December 7

Navigating plan choices does not have to be overwhelming. You can review your options, check network restrictions, and verify drug prices using free official resources.

1. **Review Your Plan's Annual Notice of Change (ANOC):** Insurance companies are required to detail all changes to premiums, deductibles, copays, and provider networks. Check this notice immediately to see if your plan is terminating on December 31 or if your current doctors and pharmacies are leaving the network.
2. **Build a Drug Profile on Medicare.gov:** Use the official Medicare Plan Finder tool at medicare.gov/plan-compare. Log into your account and enter your exact prescription names, dosages, and preferred pharmacies. The system calculates your total expected out-of-pocket costs for the year across every plan available in your zip code.
3. **Contact 1-800-MEDICARE:** If you do not have internet access or prefer speaking directly with a representative, call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227). Federal representatives are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, to provide objective comparisons of local options.
4. **Get Free, Local Guidance from SHIP:** The State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) provides free, unbiased, one-on-one counseling from certified local experts who do not sell insurance. You can connect with your local SHIP counselor by visiting shiphelp.org or calling 1-877-839-2675.

## Action Checklist for the Final Two Weeks

With only 14 days remaining until the December 7 cutoff, work through these concrete steps to ensure your healthcare remains protected for 2027:

- ☐ Gather all current prescription bottles to record exact medication names, dosages, and refill frequencies.
- ☐ Check whether your existing Medicare Advantage or Part D plan is among the policies terminating on December 31.
- ☐ Log into Medicare.gov to enter your medications and verify which local pharmacies offer preferred pricing.
- ☐ Verify that your primary care physicians, specialists, and hospital systems remain inside your plan's network for 2027.
- ☐ Confirm that your estimated annual prescription costs remain within the new $2,400 maximum spending limit.
- ☐ Reach out to an unbiased SHIP counselor at shiphelp.org or 1-877-839-2675 if you need guidance evaluating plan alternatives.
- ☐ Submit your final plan selection online at Medicare.gov or by phone before midnight on Monday, December 7.

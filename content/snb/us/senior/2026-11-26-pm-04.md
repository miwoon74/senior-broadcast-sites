## Why these features matter for older visitors

- Designated senior evenings and timed tickets reduce crowding and make walking at a gentle pace more comfortable.
- Paved, well‑lit loops and benches at regular intervals lower the physical barrier for people who use walkers or wheelchairs or who need frequent rests.
- Accessible restrooms and nearby drop‑off/parking reduce walking distance and make planning easier for caregivers and group leaders.
- Guided slow‑walk tours give structure and reassurance for visitors who prefer a paced, supervised experience.

## How to plan a comfortable visit — step by step

1. Visit the Desert Garden official events page and reserve a timed senior ticket for one of the designated senior evenings in Nov.–Dec. 2026. (The announcement was posted Nov. 24, 2026.)
2. Read the venue’s accessibility notes on the same page: confirm paved loops, benches, accessible restrooms, and the location of accessible parking or drop‑off points.
3. If you need mobility assistance, call the garden ahead to ask about shuttle/drop‑off options and the availability of guided slow‑walk tours.
4. Arrange transport early — check whether your local senior center or Area Agency on Aging runs shuttle trips or group outings for seasonal events.
5. On the night: arrive early for easy parking/drop‑off, use benches to rest frequently, and stick to the paved, well‑lit loops.

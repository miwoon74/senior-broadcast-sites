A fall can change daily life in an instant, yet many of the risks inside a home are easy to spot and cheap to fix. Walk through your home this week with this short list.

## The five checks

1. **Loose rugs.** Tape them down with double-sided rug tape, or put them away.
2. **Night lighting.** Plug-in night lights in the hallway and bathroom make the trip at 3 a.m. much safer.
3. **Grab bars.** A bar beside the toilet and inside the shower gives you something solid to hold. Towel racks are not built to carry your weight.
4. **Clear walkways.** Move cords, shoes and stacks of newspapers out of the paths you use every day.
5. **Shoes with grip.** Slippers without backs or with smooth soles are a common cause of slips.

## One more conversation

Some medicines can cause dizziness, especially when they are combined. At your next checkup, ask your doctor or pharmacist to review everything you take, including vitamins.

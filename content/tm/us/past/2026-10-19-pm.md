On July 13, 1985, the Live Aid concerts took place at Wembley Stadium in London and JFK Stadium in Philadelphia. Many of the biggest acts of the day performed to raise money for famine relief in Ethiopia.

The broadcast reached a worldwide television audience, and many viewers still remember staying in front of the set all day.

On April 12, 1981, Space Shuttle Columbia lifted off from Kennedy Space Center with astronauts John Young and Robert Crippen aboard. It was the first time a spacecraft designed to be flown again went into orbit.

Two days later, Columbia glided back to a landing in California, and millions of Americans watched it touch down on television.

**Grandma Wellness Recipes** shares what grandmas around the world made, and when: the soups, teas and simple dishes, and the wisdom passed down with them. Each day we post one recipe in full, alongside a YouTube Short.

Every recipe lists how many it serves, exact amounts and every step. When old books record the food, or when studies in people have looked at it, we say so and link the source. Stars (★) show how much evidence there is, and colors (🟢🟡🔴) show how safe it is to eat.

These articles introduce traditional food customs. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Please talk to your doctor if you have health concerns. Articles are written with the help of AI tools and checked before they are posted.

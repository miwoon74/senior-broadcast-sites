Creamy risotto loaded with browned mushrooms. Keep the broth hot and stir often; it takes about 30 minutes.

> This is the dish from the “Eating out anyway?” corner of our November 25 grandma recipe video. Grab it at a restaurant, or make it at home when you have time.

## Ingredients (4 servings)

| Ingredients | Amount |
|---|---|
| Arborio rice | 1 1/2 cups (300g) |
| Mixed mushrooms | 1 lb (450g) |
| Onion, finely chopped | 1 |
| Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| Chicken or vegetable broth, hot | 5 cups (1.2 L) |
| Dry white wine (optional) | 1/2 cup (120 ml) |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Butter | 2 tbsp |
| Grated Parmesan | 1/2 cup (50g) |
| Salt and pepper | to taste |
| Parsley | to serve |

## Steps

1. Brown the sliced mushrooms in 1 tbsp oil over high heat, season with salt, and set aside.
2. In the same pot, cook the onion in the remaining oil for 5 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Add the rice and stir 2 minutes until the edges look clear. Add the wine (if using) and stir until absorbed.
4. Add the hot broth one ladle at a time, stirring until each is absorbed, for 18–20 minutes, until the rice is tender but still a little firm.
5. Stir in the mushrooms, butter and Parmesan. Season and finish with parsley.

## Grandma's tips

- Don't crowd the mushrooms in the pan or they will steam instead of brown.
- Serve right away; risotto thickens as it sits.

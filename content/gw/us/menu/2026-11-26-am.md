Yogurt-marinated chicken in a creamy, turmeric-golden tomato sauce. A takeout favorite you can make in one pan.

> This is the dish from the “Eating out anyway?” corner of our November 26 grandma recipe video. Grab it at a restaurant, or make it at home when you have time.

## Ingredients (4 servings)

| Ingredients | Amount |
|---|---|
| Boneless chicken thighs | 1 1/2 lb (680g) |
| Plain yogurt | 1/2 cup (120g) |
| Ground turmeric | 1 1/2 tsp |
| Garam masala | 3 tsp |
| Ground cumin | 1 tsp |
| Chili powder | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1 1/2 tsp |
| Butter | 2 tbsp |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 1 tbsp |
| Tomato puree | 1 can (14 oz / 400g) |
| Heavy cream | 1/2 cup (120 ml) |

## Steps

1. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces. Mix with yogurt, 1 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp garam masala, cumin, chili powder and 1 tsp salt. Marinate 30 minutes.
2. Broil or pan-sear the chicken over high heat until charred in spots. Set aside.
3. Melt the butter in the pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes. Add garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
4. Add the tomato puree, the remaining turmeric, garam masala and salt. Simmer 10 minutes.
5. Stir in the cream and chicken and simmer 5 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through. Serve with rice or naan.

## Grandma's tips

- Thighs stay juicier than breast meat.
- For a milder sauce, skip the chili powder.

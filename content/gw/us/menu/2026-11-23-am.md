A classic double-crust apple pie. Store-bought crust keeps it easy; the filling is just apples, sugar and cinnamon.

> This is the dish from the “Eating out anyway?” corner of our November 23 grandma recipe video. Grab it at a restaurant, or make it at home when you have time.

## Ingredients (one 9-inch pie)

| Ingredients | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pie crusts (store-bought, for a double-crust pie) | 2 |
| Apples | 6 (about 2 lb / 900g) |
| Sugar | 1/2 cup (100g) |
| Ground cinnamon | 1 tsp |
| Ground nutmeg | 1/4 tsp |
| Lemon juice | 1 tbsp |
| Cornstarch | 2 tbsp |
| Butter | 2 tbsp |
| Egg (for brushing) | 1 |

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Fit one crust into a 9-inch pie pan.
2. Peel and slice the apples 1/4 inch thick. Toss with sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, lemon juice and cornstarch.
3. Pile the apples into the crust and dot with butter.
4. Cover with the second crust, crimp the edges and cut 4 slits on top. Brush with beaten egg.
5. Bake 20 minutes, then lower to 375°F (190°C) and bake 35–40 minutes more, until golden and bubbling.
6. Cool at least 2 hours before slicing.

## Grandma's tips

- A mix of tart and sweet apples gives the best flavor.
- Cover the edges with foil if they brown too fast.

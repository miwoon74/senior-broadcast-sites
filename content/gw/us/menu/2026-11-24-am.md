Black tea simmered with whole spices and finished with hot milk. Five minutes, and it tastes like the coffee shop.

> This is the dish from the “Eating out anyway?” corner of our November 24 grandma recipe video. Grab it at a restaurant, or make it at home when you have time.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

| Ingredients | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water | 1 cup (240 ml) |
| Black tea bags | 2 |
| Milk | 1 cup (240 ml) |
| Cinnamon stick | 1 |
| Whole cloves | 4 |
| Cardamom pods, crushed | 4 |
| Fresh ginger slices | 4 |
| Honey or sugar | 1–2 tbsp |

## Steps

1. Simmer the water with cinnamon, cloves, cardamom and ginger for 3 minutes.
2. Add the tea bags and steep 2 minutes off the heat.
3. Remove the tea bags, add the milk and heat until steaming (do not boil).
4. Sweeten with honey or sugar, strain into mugs, and froth the top if you like.

## Grandma's tips

- Shake the warm milk in a lidded jar for a quick foam.
- Do not give honey to babies under 1 year old.

A clear beef broth scented with charred ginger, cinnamon and star anise. The broth simmers for over an hour, but your hands-on time is about 20 minutes.

> This is the dish from the “Eating out anyway?” corner of our November 22 grandma recipe video. Grab it at a restaurant, or make it at home when you have time.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

| Ingredients | Amount |
|---|---|
| Beef brisket (for broth) | 10 oz (300g) |
| Thinly sliced raw beef (topping) | 3.5 oz (100g) |
| Rice noodles | 7 oz (200g) |
| Onion | 1 |
| Fresh ginger | 1 oz (30g) |
| Cinnamon stick | 1 |
| Star anise | 2 |
| Whole cloves | 3 |
| Water | 8 cups (2 L) |
| Fish sauce | 3 tbsp |
| Sugar | 1 tsp |
| Salt | to taste |
| Bean sprouts | 3.5 oz (100g) |
| Scallions, cilantro, lime | to serve |

## Steps

1. Halve the onion and ginger (skin on) and char them in a dry pan until blackened.
2. Toast the cinnamon, star anise and cloves in a dry pan for 1 minute.
3. Blanch the brisket in boiling water for 5 minutes and rinse.
4. Put 8 cups water, brisket, onion, ginger and spices in a pot. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently for 1 1/2 hours, skimming the foam.
5. Strain the broth and season with 3 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tsp sugar and salt. Slice the brisket thinly.
6. Soak the noodles in warm water for 30 minutes, dip in boiling water for 20 seconds and divide between bowls.
7. Top with brisket, raw beef and sprouts, then pour boiling broth over to cook the beef. Serve with scallions, cilantro and lime.

## Grandma's tips

- Charring the onion and ginger gives the broth its deep color and aroma.
- Freeze extra broth in portions for a 10-minute bowl next time.

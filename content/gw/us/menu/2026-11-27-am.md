Fresh apple cider simmered with cinnamon, cloves and orange. Make a pot and the whole house smells like the holidays.

> This is the dish from the “Eating out anyway?” corner of our November 27 grandma recipe video. Grab it at a restaurant, or make it at home when you have time.

## Ingredients (4 servings)

| Ingredients | Amount |
|---|---|
| Apple cider (unfiltered apple juice) | 4 cups (1 L) |
| Cinnamon sticks | 2 |
| Whole cloves | 6 |
| Star anise | 1 |
| Orange, sliced | 1/2 |
| Maple syrup (optional) | 1–2 tbsp |

## Steps

1. Put the cider, cinnamon, cloves, star anise and orange slices in a pot.
2. Heat until steaming, then keep at a bare simmer for 15 minutes (do not boil).
3. Taste and add maple syrup if you like it sweeter.
4. Strain into mugs and garnish with a cinnamon stick.

## Grandma's tips

- A slow cooker on low keeps it warm for a party.
- Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge; reheat gently.

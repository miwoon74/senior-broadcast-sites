After a big dinner, when the stomach felt heavy, grandmas in Japan's apple country simmered apples and drank them like tea. Three ingredients, one small pot, and about 10 minutes.

## Where It Comes From
Ringo-cha (りんご茶, "apple tea") is a simple drink from apple-growing regions of Japan such as Aomori, where apples are simmered in water and sipped like tea. In apple country, people have long made it after a meal when the stomach felt heavy.
Apples themselves were loved enough to earn famous sayings: "An apple a day keeps the doctor away," and in Japan, "When apples turn red, doctors turn pale."
This isn't the bagged herbal tea from the store: it's real apple slices simmered in water.

## Ingredients (Serves 1)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Apple, thinly sliced, skin on | 100 g (about 3½ oz) |
| Water | 300 ml (about 1¼ cups) |
| Honey (optional) | 7 g (1 tsp) |

## How to Make It
1. Thinly slice the apple, leaving the skin on.
2. Put the slices in a small pot with the 300 ml of water and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Strain into a cup and stir in the honey. (You can eat the soft apple slices as they are.)

## Why People Love It
- A warm cup that helps you get fluids
- Studies in children suggest honey may help ease coughs

## What Science Says
Apples contain fiber such as pectin, but no study has looked directly at apple tea and digestion. Science hasn't tested it yet — that's not the same as wrong. And Grandma had good reasons: a warm cup after a big meal is a gentle way to get fluids and slow down.

## Today's Rating
| Evidence | Safety |
|---|---|
| ★ (a remedy passed down through generations) | 🟢 (safe when enjoyed as ordinary food) |

## How to Enjoy It
After meals, 1 to 2 cups a day.

## Just One Caution
- There's no special caution for this recipe. Please just follow the general note below.

## General Note
- **Never give honey to babies under 1 year old.**
  Honey can contain spores of the bacteria that cause botulism, and babies under 1 year old can't yet fight them off. If a baby will share the drink, make it without honey.

Picture a clear, steaming broth with soft, silky rounds of taro floating on top. That's toran-guk, the soup Korean grandmothers make every harvest season. Seven ingredients, about 35 minutes of simmering, and a pot that fills your kitchen with the smell of a gentle beef broth.

## Where It Comes From

In Korea, families make this clear beef-and-taro soup around Chuseok, the autumn harvest holiday, when fresh taro arrives at the market. It's also a bowl people have long turned to when their stomach felt a little unsettled.

## Ingredients (Serves 4)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Taro root | 300 g (about 10½ oz) |
| Beef brisket or thin-sliced beef | 150 g (about 5 oz) |
| Water | 1200 ml (about 5 cups) |
| Minced garlic | 10 g (2 tsp) |
| Korean soup soy sauce (guk-ganjang) | 15 ml (1 Tbsp) |
| Salt | 2 g (to taste) |
| Green onion | 10 g (about 1 stalk) |

## How to Make It

1. Put on gloves and peel the taro.
2. Boil the taro for 5 minutes, then drain and discard the water.
3. Put the beef in the water and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Add the taro and garlic, simmer 15 minutes more, then season with the soy sauce and salt. Top with the green onion.

## What's Nice About It

- Warm broth makes it easy to get fluids.
- The beef adds protein to the bowl.
- Seven ingredients and four simple steps.

## What the Old Books Say
- **Donguibogam (Korea, 1613)**: taro is mild, eases the stomach and bowels, and though raw taro is harmful, cooked taro nourishes the body.

## What Research Says Today
We haven't found studies in people on taro soup and digestion yet, so we share this as **wisdom passed down through generations**. What's clear is that a warm, clear broth between rich holiday dishes gives you fluids and a little protein.

## How to Enjoy It

**One bowl, in the fall around harvest time.** Make a pot when fresh taro shows up and share it with the family.

## Just One Caution (Plus One More)

- **Always cook taro all the way through** — raw taro contains needle-shaped crystals called calcium oxalate that can make your mouth and throat sting and itch.
- **Wear gloves when peeling** — the same crystals can make your bare hands itchy.

Ever wondered what the stars and colored circles at the end of our videos mean? On Grandma's Wisdom, every dish gets a simple card that sums up two things: how much research backs it, and how safe it is. Here's how to read it.

## About This Series
We share one dish or drink at a time, handed down in grandmothers' kitchens around the world. Weekdays bring a new recipe, Saturdays a world comparison, and Sundays a recap of the week.

## Stars = Evidence

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ★ | Traditional use only / lab or animal research level |
| ★★ | Some research, but results are limited or mixed |
| ★★★ | Confirmed in studies with people |

One star doesn't mean a food is bad. It means people have loved it for a long time, but science hasn't confirmed it yet.

## Colors = Safety

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 | Safe when eaten in normal food amounts |
| 🟡 | Take care with large amounts or certain people (such as those on medication) |
| 🔴 | Shared for the story only — please don't try it |

## Let's Read an Example
If a card shows **Evidence ★ / Safety 🟢**, it means: "Not proven by science, but perfectly fine to enjoy as food." Go ahead and savor it.

## The One Caution
When you see a 🟡 yellow card, please read the "one caution" in the video and blog all the way to the end. It means there's something to watch for if you eat a lot of it or take certain medicines. 🔴 Red is for enjoying the old story only.

## Sources
The grading scale is this series' own standard. Sources for each dish are listed in full on that episode's blog post.

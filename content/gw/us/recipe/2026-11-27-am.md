On a chilly evening, right after supper, do you remember Grandma simmering apple slices with a cinnamon stick until the whole kitchen smelled sweet? Today it's Germany's winter favorite, Apfel-Zimt-Tee: apple cinnamon tea. A quarter of an apple, one cinnamon stick, and 5 minutes. That's all.

## Where It Comes From

Apfel-Zimt-Tee simply means "apple cinnamon tea" in German. Despite the name, there are no tea leaves in it. It's a fruit tea made by simmering or steeping apple and cinnamon in hot water. German families have long enjoyed a warm cup after meals on cold winter days.

Old books mention apple, too. The Dongui Bogam (1613), Korea's trusted medical classic written by a royal doctor, says "it eases stomach upsets and belly aches and clears phlegm" (治霍亂肚痛 消痰). The Bencao Gangmu (1578), a classic Chinese book on medicinal plants, says "it settles the stomach and clears phlegm" (下氣消痰). And for the cinnamon that goes in, the Dongui Bogam says "it warms the middle and opens the blood vessels" (主溫中 通血脉).

## Ingredients (Serves 1)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Apple | 50 g (about ¼ apple, thinly sliced) |
| Cinnamon stick | 1 (or 0.5 g ground cinnamon, ¼ tsp) |
| Hot water | 200 ml (about ⅞ cup) |
| Honey (optional) | 7 g (1 tsp) |

*1 tbsp = 15 ml, 1 tsp = 5 ml*

## How to Make It

1. Thinly slice 50 g (about ¼) of an apple.
2. Put the apple, 1 cinnamon stick, and 200 ml of water in a small pot and simmer for 5 minutes. No pot? Put the apple and cinnamon in a mug, pour in hot water, and steep for 10 minutes. Sweet apple and warm cinnamon will fill the air.
3. Strain, then stir in 1 tsp (7 g) of honey if you like.

## The Good Parts

- A warm cup helps you get fluids.
- Honey may help ease coughs, according to studies in children.

## What Science Says

Cinnamon hasn't been clearly shown to help with diabetes, weight, or any other health condition. But not yet confirmed is not the same as wrong. In normal food amounts, cinnamon is generally considered safe. A warm cup after supper on a cold evening, to warm up and get some fluids — that's likely why Grandma made it.

## Today's Rating

- Evidence: ★ (A remedy passed down through generations)
- Safety: 🟢 (Safe in normal food amounts)

## How to Enjoy It

On chilly days, enjoy 1 to 2 cups a day.

## Please Note

- **Don't have lots of cinnamon for a long time — especially if you have liver disease** — Common cassia cinnamon contains a compound called coumarin, which can burden the liver in large amounts. Ceylon cinnamon contains almost no coumarin.

## General Precautions

- **Never give honey to babies under 1 year old** — Honey can contain botulism spores, and babies under 1 year old can't yet fight them off.

When the autumn air turns dry and your throat feels scratchy, do you remember the sweet soup Grandma simmered from a white mushroom? Today it's China's autumn dessert, yin er tang (银耳汤), snow fungus soup. Soak, tear, and simmer on low. That's all.

## Where It Comes From

Snow fungus is a soft white mushroom (also called white wood ear). In China, when the autumn air gets dry, families have long cooked it slowly into a sweet dessert soup. It has been passed down as a bowl that moistens the throat and skin.

Old books mention mushrooms, too. The Bencao Gangmu (1578), a classic Chinese book on medicinal plants, says "it adds strength and keeps hunger away" (益氣不飢). The Dongui Bogam (1613), Korea's most trusted medical book, written by a royal doctor, says "it cheers the spirit and opens the appetite" (悅神開胃). For the jujubes that go in, the Dongui Bogam says "it settles the middle, nourishes the stomach and strengthens the organs" (安中養脾 補五藏).

## Ingredients (Serves 4)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Dried snow fungus | 10 g (about 1/3 oz) |
| Water | 1000 ml (about 4 cups) |
| Rock sugar | 30 g (about 2 tbsp) |
| Dried jujubes (optional) | 4 |

*1 tbsp = 15 ml, 1 tsp = 5 ml*

## How to Make It

1. Soak the snow fungus in water for 1 hour. It will open up like a flower.
2. Trim off the hard base and tear it into small pieces.
3. Put 1000 ml of water, the fungus and the jujubes in a pot and simmer on low for 40 minutes. (Hands-on time: 10 minutes)
4. When the soup turns silky, stir in the rock sugar until it melts.

## The Good Parts

- It contains dietary fiber.

## What Science Says

Most research on the polysaccharides in snow fungus has been done in the lab, and there are still too few studies confirming effects in people. But not yet tested is not the same as wrong. A warm, silky bowl in dry autumn weather helps you get some fluids. That's likely why Grandma made it.

## Today's Rating

- Evidence: ★ (A remedy passed down through generations)
- Safety: 🟢 (Safe in normal food amounts)

## How to Enjoy It

In dry autumn weather, enjoy about 1 bowl a day.

On bloated, heavy mornings, grandmas in India made a warm cup of coriander seed water. Just two ingredients, and it's ready in 5 minutes.

## Where it comes from
"Dhaniya pani" means "coriander water." Indian families have long drunk it in the morning, believing it helped digestion and eased puffiness — a cup for mornings when the stomach felt full and heavy.

Coriander seeds come from the same plant as cilantro. Look for them in the spice aisle.

## Ingredients (serves 1)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Coriander seeds | 5 g (about 2 tsp) |
| Water | 300 ml (about 1¼ cups) |

## How to make it
1. Lightly crush 5 g of coriander seeds with a mortar or the flat side of a knife.
2. Boil with 300 ml of water for 5 minutes (or soak in the water overnight).
3. Strain and drink.

## The good parts
- A warm drink that helps you get fluids in
- Two ingredients, about 5 minutes

## What science says
There are no studies in people on coriander seed water for digestion or puffiness yet. Science hasn't tested it yet — that's not the same as wrong. And Grandma had good reasons: a warm cup first thing in the morning is a gentle way to start the day with some fluids.

## Today's rating
| Evidence | Safety |
|---|---|
| ★ (a remedy passed down through generations) | 🟢 (safe in normal food amounts) |

## How to enjoy it
In the morning, one cup a day.

## Just be careful about this
- **Skip it if you're allergic to cilantro (coriander), celery, or carrots**
  They belong to the same plant family, so allergic reactions can show up together.

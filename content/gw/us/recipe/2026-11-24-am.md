On days when the cold wind chills you to the bone, do you remember the warm drink Grandma heated on the stove? Today's cup is grape juice gently warmed with cinnamon. All you need is juice and a few spices, and about 5 minutes at the stove.

## Where It Comes From

In Europe, people warm red wine with spices in winter and call it glühwein (German for "glow wine," a hot mulled wine). This drink is an alcohol-free twist on glühwein, made with grape juice instead. That means anyone can enjoy it, even in the evening. It's a cup for cold days when you want to warm up.

Old books mention these spices, too. The Bencao Gangmu (1578), a classic Chinese book on medicinal plants, says cinnamon "warms the middle, firms the sinews and bones and opens the blood vessels" (溫中，堅筋骨，通血脈). The Dongui Bogam (1613), Korea's most trusted medical book, written by a royal doctor, says it "warms the middle and opens the blood vessels" (主溫中 通血脉). For cloves, the Bencao Gangmu says "it warms the stomach" (溫脾胃), and the Dongui Bogam says "it warms the stomach and stops violent stomach upsets" (溫脾胃 止霍亂).

## Ingredients (Serves 1)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| 100% grape juice | 200 ml (about 7 fl oz) |
| Cinnamon stick | 1 (or 0.5 g ground cinnamon, 1/4 tsp) |
| Whole cloves (optional) | 2 |
| Orange peel (optional) | 1 strip |

## How to Make It

1. Put the grape juice and spices (cinnamon, cloves, orange peel) in a small pot.
2. Warm on low heat for 5 minutes without letting it boil. Aim for a gentle wisp of steam, not bubbles.
3. Remove the spices and pour into a mug.

## The Good Parts

- Sipping it warm helps you stay hydrated.
- It uses an ingredient that contains vitamin C.

## What Science Says

Because this is a modern twist, no one has studied what happens when grape juice and these spices are combined. Cinnamon itself hasn't been clearly shown to help with any disease. But not yet tested is not the same as wrong. A warm cup on a cold day keeps you hydrated, and the cinnamon aroma is a comfort all its own. That's likely why Grandma reached for it.

## Today's Rating

- Evidence: ★ (A remedy passed down through generations)
- Safety: 🟢 (Safe in normal food amounts)

## How to Enjoy It

On cold days when you want to warm up, enjoy about 1 mug a day.

## Please Note

- **If you have diabetes, dilute the juice about half and half with water** — 200 ml of grape juice is high in sugar and can raise blood sugar quickly.
- **Don't have a lot of cinnamon for a long time, especially if you have liver disease** — The common cassia cinnamon contains a substance called coumarin, which can strain the liver in large amounts. Ceylon cinnamon has very little coumarin.

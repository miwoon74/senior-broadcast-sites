"The day after drinking, have clam miso soup." That's what people in Japan have long said. In Korea, grandmas made a clear soybean sprout soup, and in France, early risers at the Paris market warmed up with onion soup. Here are the recipes and the stories behind three morning-after soups.

## 1. Japan: Shijimi Clam Miso Soup (しじみ汁)

### Where it comes from
In Japan, clam miso soup is the classic bowl for the day after drinking. Shijimi are tiny freshwater clams, and the shijimi soup made with clams from Lake Shinji in Shimane is known as a regional specialty.

### Ingredients (serves 2)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Shijimi clams (cleaned of sand) | 200 g (about 7 oz) |
| Water | 400 ml (about 1⅔ cups) |
| Miso | 24 g (1½ tablespoons) |
| Green onion | 5 g |

### How to make it
1. Soak the clams in 1% salt water for 1 to 3 hours so they release their sand.
2. Rub the shells together to wash them, then put them in a pot starting with cold water.
3. When the shells open, lower the heat and dissolve the miso.
4. Add the green onion.

### What's nice about it
- You get some protein along with the broth.

### Today's grade
Evidence ★ traditional use only · Safety 🟢 safe in normal food amounts · Suggested: one bowl the day after drinking

### Just be careful
- **Skip it if you have a shellfish allergy** — shellfish are one of the most common food allergens.
- **Cook until every shell opens** — undercooked clams can carry bacteria or viruses that cause food poisoning.

## 2. Korea: Soybean Sprout Soup (Kongnamul-guk)

### Where it comes from
In Korea, this clear soybean sprout soup is the most popular "haejang-guk," the soup people eat the morning after drinking. Families also make it when someone feels a cold coming on.

### Ingredients (serves 2)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Soybean sprouts | 200 g (about 7 oz) |
| Water | 800 ml (about 3⅓ cups) |
| Minced garlic | 5 g (1 teaspoon) |
| Salt | 3 g (½ teaspoon, adjust to taste) |
| Green onion | 10 g |

### How to make it
1. When the water boils, add the sprouts, cover, and cook 5 to 7 minutes (don't lift the lid partway).
2. Season with the garlic and salt.
3. Add the green onion and turn off the heat.

### What's nice about it
- A small Korean study found that asparagine from soybean sprouts lowered blood alcohol faster.

### Today's grade
Evidence ★★ some research, limited or mixed results · Safety 🟢 safe in normal food amounts · Suggested: one bowl the day after drinking

## 3. France: Onion Soup (Soupe à l'oignon)

### Where it comes from
At Les Halles, the old central market of Paris, night-shift workers and late-night drinkers ate this soup at dawn. It's also a favorite on cold days.

### Ingredients (serves 2)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Onions | 400 g (2 onions, about 14 oz) |
| Butter | 10 g (just under 1 tablespoon) |
| Water | 600 ml (about 2½ cups) |
| Bouillon cube | 5 g (1 cube) |
| Salt and pepper | 1 g (to taste) |

### How to make it
1. Slice the onions thinly. (Microwaving them for 5 minutes shortens the cooking time.)
2. Cook in butter for 15 to 20 minutes until brown.
3. Add the water and bouillon, simmer 10 minutes, and season.

### What's nice about it
- The warm broth helps you get fluids back in.
- Slow-cooked onions turn sweet and delicious.

### Today's grade
Evidence ★ traditional use only · Safety 🟢 safe in normal food amounts · Suggested: one bowl the day after drinking

### Just be careful
- **With high blood pressure, use only half the bouillon cube** — bouillon cubes are high in salt, and salt can raise blood pressure.

## What science says
- **Clam miso soup**: Shijimi clams are known to contain amino acids such as ornithine, but we found no reliable human studies on the soup itself. Enjoy it as wisdom passed down through generations.
- **Soybean sprout soup**: In a small Korean study, soups with more sprouts had more asparagine, and blood alcohol reached zero faster in the group that had asparagine. It was a small, preliminary study.
- **Onion soup**: There are no studies on it directly. Enjoy it as a warm bowl that brings back fluids and salt.

Of the three, the soybean sprout soup (★★) has the most research behind it.

> **General caution**
> - With high blood pressure, keep it lightly salted and have less broth — too much salt can raise blood pressure.

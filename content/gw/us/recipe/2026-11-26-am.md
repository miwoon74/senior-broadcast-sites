When a cold wind leaves you shivery and you feel a cold coming on, nothing beats a warm bowl of soup. Today's recipe is rasam, the sour, peppery soup South Indian grandmothers pour over rice when someone catches a cold. One pot, 10 minutes of boiling.

## Where It Comes From

Rasam is a home-style soup from South India. When someone caught a cold or felt chilled, families poured this tangy, pepper-heavy broth over rice. The sourness comes from tamarind, a tangy fruit sold as a paste. Look for it at Indian or Asian grocery stores, or online.

## Ingredients (Serves 4)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tamarind paste | 15 g (1 tbsp) |
| Tomatoes | 200 g (2 medium) |
| Water | 800 ml (about 3⅓ cups) |
| Black pepper | 2 g (1 tsp) |
| Cumin | 2 g (1 tsp) |
| Garlic | 10 g (2 cloves) |
| Ground turmeric | 0.5 g (a pinch) |
| Salt | 5 g (1 tsp) |
| Cooking oil | 10 ml (2 tsp) |
| Mustard seeds (optional) | 2 g (½ tsp) |

## How to Make It

1. Crush the black pepper, cumin, and garlic with a mortar and pestle. The aroma hits right away.
2. Put 800 ml of water, the tamarind paste, tomatoes, crushed spices, turmeric, and salt in a pot and boil for 10 minutes.
3. In a small pan, fry the mustard seeds in the oil, then pour it into the soup.

## The Good Parts

- A warm broth helps you get fluids in, too.
- Sour and spicy, it helps rice go down when you've lost your appetite.

## What Science Says

We couldn't find any studies that looked directly at rasam and colds. That only means it hasn't been tested yet — not that it's wrong. A hot, spicy broth warms you up, and it makes a bowl of rice easy to eat on a day with no appetite. That's likely why Grandma reached for it.

## How to Enjoy It

When a cold is coming on, pour one warm bowl over rice.

## Just One Caution

- **If you have gastritis or acid reflux, use less pepper** — spicy seasonings like pepper can irritate the stomach and esophagus.

## General Note

- **If you have high blood pressure, keep it lightly salted and go easy on the broth** — eating a lot of salt can raise blood pressure.

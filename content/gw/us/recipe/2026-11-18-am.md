On chilly days, when a scratchy throat crept in, Grandma would drop sliced apple and ginger into a little pot and let it simmer. Sweet apple, a gentle bite of ginger, and a slice of lemon at the end. One pot and 10 minutes make a warm cup for two.

## Where It Comes From
Apple ginger lemon tea is a homestyle autumn drink made by simmering apple, ginger, and lemon together. Rather than one country's ancient recipe, it's the kind of cup people have long made from what's in the kitchen when apples are in season. It has been sipped warm on chilly days, when your throat feels scratchy.

## Ingredients (Serves 2)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Apple, thinly sliced | 100 g (3.5 oz) |
| Fresh ginger, thinly sliced | 10 g |
| Lemon slices | 2 |
| Water | 500 ml (about 2 cups) |
| Honey (optional) | 14 g (2 tsp) |

## How to Make It
1. Thinly slice 100 g (3.5 oz) of apple and 10 g of fresh ginger.
2. Put 500 ml (about 2 cups) of water, the apple, and the ginger in a pot and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Add 2 lemon slices and turn off the heat.
4. Strain into cups and, if you like, stir in 14 g (2 tsp) of honey.

## Why People Love It
- There is human research suggesting ginger can ease nausea
- Sipping it warm is an easy way to get fluids in

## What Science Says
No studies have looked at this particular blend of apple, ginger, and lemon. Ginger on its own, though, has been studied, and studies suggest it may ease nausea. Science hasn't tested this cup yet — that's not the same as wrong. And Grandma had a good reason to make it: a warm drink on a chilly day is a simple, pleasant way to get fluids in.

| Evidence | Safety |

## How to Enjoy It
Sip 1–2 cups a day, warm and slow, on chilly days.

## One Thing to Watch
- **Use less ginger if you get heartburn or an upset stomach**
  For some people, ginger can cause mild side effects such as heartburn or diarrhea.

## General Caution
- **Never give honey to babies under 1 year old**
  Honey can contain spores of the bacteria that cause botulism, and babies under 1 can't yet fight them off. Leave the honey out if a baby will be sharing.
